Six additions bring the catalog from 38 to 44 cases: 5 official showcases and 39 community projects/experiments. Three existing cases receive material updates. ## Discovery and coverage

GitHub repository search used `"rea" "reverse"`, ordered by recent update (50 results). The latest 25 upstream discussions by creation date were screened; #8070 (camera), #7989 (Eleven) and #7974 (App X-Ray) supplied author-owned repository leads. This is a selected update, not exhaustive discovery or an ecosystem ranking. Search indexing, limits and selection leave gaps.

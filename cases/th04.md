## The question

How are bullets evenly distributed around a ring and aimed toward the player?

## What REA did

REA and Ghidra check the loading layout, analyze helper functions and read instructions and raw bytes. The existing reconstruction project supplies context for fixed-point angles, integer division and wrapping.

## Author-reported results

The showcase presents new REA checks alongside historical Turbo C++ verification: a 2,139-byte region containing the helper matches.

## Limitations and unverified claims

The historical source reconstruction and compilation checks belong to the TH04 project. The new REA investigation is only part of the evidence, not proof of full-game equivalence or sole REA authorship.

## What to learn

Confirm the loading model and address mapping on old platforms, then connect algorithm explanations to compiler checks.

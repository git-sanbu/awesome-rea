# Research and source records

[简体中文](SOURCES.zh-CN.md) · [Catalog](README.md)

Updated **2026-10-21**. Each entry retains its own source-review date. This collection concerns `morluto/rea` (npm `rea-agents`), not unrelated projects sharing the name REA.

## Method

- Search GitHub projects and issues for `"rea-agents"`, `"morluto/rea"`, `"with REA"` and `"using REA"`.
- Read the official README, showcases and public Evidence notes.
- Use web results, news and community catalogs as discovery leads, then read the original author's sources.
- Prefer complete commit-SHA links, retain project entry points and distinguish editable discussions from pinned files.
- Do not install the included third-party software or independently rerun author tests during source review.

## Discovery and acknowledgments

[AIshihzong/awesome-REA](https://github.com/AIshihzong/awesome-REA) helped identify community projects. This catalog independently reviews primary sources and writes its own summaries; it does not copy that catalog's rankings, traffic numbers or unconfirmed attributions.

Official cases were discovered through [REA Showcases](https://rea.tools/showcase/) and the [upstream repository](https://github.com/morluto/rea). Credit belongs to each original author.

Eight cases were added; two ReaFuse examples count as one project. Sixteen existing community READMEs were compared: twelve were unchanged, three had site/tool/license changes, and rea-lab added Studies 4 and 5 and a Study 3 correction. The five official cases retain their 2026-10-16 review dates.

Discussions #2254 and #3098 are primary experiment reports but editable, without immutable commit links. Historical versions and tool counts are not current-release guarantees. ## Exclusions and coverage

Mirrors, backups, generic introductions and proposed integrations without public practice evidence are excluded. Claude/Codex with IDA/Ghidra does not by itself establish REA use. A “complete clone” claim needs inspectable primary evidence. This is manually selected, limited coverage, not an exhaustive ecosystem ranking. Original-source suggestions are welcome.
